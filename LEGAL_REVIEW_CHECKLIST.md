# Legal review checklist

Internal document. Obtain qualified legal review before representing the website terms as attorney-approved.

- Customer content ownership and permitted processing
- AI and rules-based processing disclosures
- Third-party subprocessors and cross-border transfers
- Retention, deletion, backups, and legal holds
- SMS consent records, STOP/HELP handling, and messaging-provider terms
- Billing, renewal, cancellation, refunds, taxes, and chargebacks
- Security-incident notice and response responsibilities
- Service availability, maintenance, and support commitments
- Company and customer intellectual property
- Warranties, disclaimers, indemnity, and limitation of liability
- Governing law, venue, disputes, and electronic notices
- Children’s data and age restrictions
- State privacy rights and request-verification process
- Permit Command professional-scope and government-decision disclaimers
