# The Command Suite — full-site audit and optimization record

Audit date: September 15, 2026

## Executive verdict

The public site now presents one clear software offer and one distinct managed-service offer:

- **The Command Suite:** a $79/month founding pilot for one defined field-to-office workflow.
- **Permit Command:** a human-accountable permit-coordination service with three monthly active-permit tiers.

The strongest conversion path is now: recognize the paperwork problem → inspect the four-step workflow → try the product preview → understand scope and price → request a fit review. Claims remain deliberately conservative because the business does not yet have verified customer outcomes or native-integration partnerships to publish.

## 1. Content and product story

### Locked public message

**Primary promise:** Turn the texts, voice notes, emails, photos, and files crews already send into structured, approval-ready office records.

**Audience:** Owner-led contractors and small construction teams with recurring field-to-office handoffs.

**Problems addressed:** Missing or incomplete change orders, daily logs, receipt records, job-photo context, permit documents, and approvals.

**Workflow:** Capture → Structure → Clarify → Approve + Route.

**Boundary:** The Command Suite is not a CRM, accounting platform, scheduler, estimating platform, or autonomous replacement for human approval.

### Contradictions removed or controlled

- Native QuickBooks, Housecall Pro, JobTread, or Buildertrend connections are not claimed.
- Assisted routes are explicitly scoped account by account.
- Permit Command is separated from the software pilot and makes no approval or government-timeline guarantee.
- Illustrative demos are labeled as samples, not customer outcomes.
- Unverified testimonials, customer logos, scarcity, and savings statistics remain excluded.

## 2. Brand voice

The approved voice is concise, contractor-specific, operational, and evidence-led. Generic language about “revolutionizing” construction or “AI-powered transformation” is intentionally excluded. The final design system uses warm white, blush quartz, rosewater, champagne gold, muted coral, soft taupe, and warm charcoal. Legacy orange and green values are overridden by the shared brand tokens.

## 4. SEO and route strategy

- Canonical origin is `https://thecommandsuite.com` throughout the public metadata.
- Organization, WebSite, SoftwareApplication, FAQPage, and Permit Command Service structured data are present.
- `robots.txt` points to the sitemap and excludes API, onboarding, login, and private Command Inbox routes.
- The sitemap prioritizes the homepage, pilot conversion content, Permit Command, and the three primary workflow pages.
- Three thin duplicate keyword routes permanently redirect to the stronger canonical pages:
  - `/construction-daily-log-automation` → `/contractor-daily-logs`
  - `/construction-receipt-tracking` → `/contractor-receipt-capture`
  - `/contractor-change-order-workflow` → `/construction-change-orders`
- Customer and owner workspaces are explicitly marked `noindex`.

## 5. Accessibility and mobile checks

- One H1 is used on each public marketing page.
- Skip navigation is present on the homepage and lead magnet.
- Form fields have labels, required states, autocomplete hints, live error messaging, and honeypot protection.
- Focus styling and reduced-motion handling are defined.
- Regular navigation and action text now has a 14px floor; dense secondary labels have a 12px floor.
- The four-step workflow collapses to a single column on narrow screens.
- Mobile hero and section headings use bounded responsive sizes to prevent clipping.
- Gold-on-white is not relied on for long body copy; charcoal carries primary reading text.

## 6. Technical and measurement checks

- Demo completion, sample download, form submission, login, pilot-fit, and key CTA events are instrumented through `/api/events`.
- Lead and checklist submissions have server routes and owner-desk views.
- The Command Inbox requires an authenticated user and is excluded from indexing.
- The production build completes successfully with the current application routes and API handlers.

## 7. Corrected section copy — source of truth

### Hero

**Turn scattered field updates into records your office can actually use.**

The Command Suite turns the updates your crews already send into organized change orders, daily logs, receipts, job photos, and approval-ready records.

### Product boundary

**Not another place to manage work.**

Keep your accounting, project management, email, and storage. The Command Suite focuses on the expensive gap: updates that arrive incomplete, live in the wrong thread, and get entered twice—or never.

### Workflow

**Capture once. Command the rest.**

Capture the source, structure supported facts, request only missing details, then require a person to approve the practical route.

### Founding pilot

**Start with one painful workflow.**

For $79/month, the founding pilot maps one defined handoff, configures one feasible intake channel, establishes required fields and missing-detail questions, names one human approver, and prepares one practical destination route. Activation is targeted within 3–7 business days after required details and access are complete.

### Permit Command

**Permit coordination with a person accountable.**

Permit Command organizes the application, required documents, status follow-up, and revision trail for an agreed permit scope. It does not guarantee approval, review speed, or jurisdiction outcomes.
